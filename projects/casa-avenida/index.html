Casa Avenida

Website · Delray Beach, FL

Marketing and sales website for Casa Avenida — 8 townhome residences at 102 SE 5th Ave, Delray Beach, completion expected Q2 2027. Sales through TJ Verdiglione and Nicole Melveney at Douglas Elliman.

The site covers per-unit floorplans with zoom/pan viewers, finish packages, a gallery, neighborhood map, and inquiry handling with spam protection and lead logging.
