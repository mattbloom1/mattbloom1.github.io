The closing page, four ways

Alternative designs for the last page of the property brochure, each built as a proof sheet showing the same design four times — both paper sizes, with two agents and with one. Nothing here is wired into the builder yet.

Front runner

Editorial, mixed sign-off

Editorial's composition — navy, portrait rail, hung typographic column — carrying the light variant's mixed sign-off: "Thank you." set in roman against a true Fraunces italic, with the sky accent on the italic word.

The two below are the originals, kept for comparison.
One

Editorial

Stays on navy but drops the symmetry. Portrait rail down the left with cut-outs running off the trim, narrow typographic column on the right, hairlines organising everything.

 Two

Light / inverted

Warm paper with navy ink. One very large sign-off, cut-outs standing on the paper with no card or shadow, and a full-bleed navy footer band as the only dark anchor.

Known flaw: the second agent block overhangs the right trim by ~0.03in. Three

Architectural

Interlocking navy and paper blocks meeting on hard edges, a drawn column grid, and the mesh-gradient monogram bleeding off the right edge. Boldest, and lightest on ink.

Worth knowing before choosing

  • All three need their own agent-card markup, because P.agentCards() and P.linkCols() are shared verbatim with the Seller Pitch and Buyer Package. Whichever wins, the brochure's closing page will visibly diverge from the other two decks.
  • The architectural proof was built at final printed size rather than the builder's 11in-wide design canvas, so its half-letter case needs re-checking during integration.
  • Each design is print-first: real inch and point units, no JavaScript needed for layout.
